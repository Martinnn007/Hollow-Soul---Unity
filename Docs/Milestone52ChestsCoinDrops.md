# M52: Chests + Coin Drops

M52 makes standard-room sustain rewards clearer and more tactile.

- Standard combat/reward room roll: 2% Golden Chest, 12% Normal Chest, 38% loose coins, 24% HP refill, 24% nothing.
- Normal Chests open with Interact and grant either 8-14 coins or HP refill.
- Golden Chests open with Interact and can grant better coins, HP plus coins, or a passive/consumable card plus coins.
- Copper, Silver, and Gold coins are physical pickups worth 1, 5, and 10 coins.
- Coins collect by walk-over proximity.
- Room Designer chest markers guide post-clear chest placement only; they never force chest kind or contents.
- Starter/origin rooms remain empty: no enemies, rewards, hazards, or chests.

Production art can replace `AP_ChestNormal`, `AP_ChestGolden`, `AP_CoinCopper`, `AP_CoinSilver`, and `AP_CoinGold` through the existing ArtPass direct-replacement workflow.
