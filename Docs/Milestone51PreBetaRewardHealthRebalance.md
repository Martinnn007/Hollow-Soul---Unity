# M51: Pre-Beta Reward + Health Rebalance

M51 tightens the normal-run reward economy before the beta vertical-slice lock. Ordinary combat/reward rooms now produce sparse sustain/economy outcomes only: small coins, HP refill, a simple treasure chest placeholder, or nothing.

Build-changing rewards are intentionally reserved for treasure rooms, boss rewards, and hub shops. This includes passive items, passive cards, active items, consumable cards, weapons, armor, and set pieces.

Balanced now starts with 3 max HP. Heavy is rescaled to 5 max HP while keeping its slower, tougher melee identity. Challenge HP penalties are clamped at runtime so curated challenges remain playable after the lower health baseline.

The M51 treasure chest is a simple reward pickup/card that grants extra coins. It is not a key system, locked chest, or interactable container yet.
